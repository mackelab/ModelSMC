- **V**: membrane voltage ← I_Na(m,h), I_K(n), I_leak, I_KM(p), I_h(r), I_inj, noise
- **m, h**: Na⁺ activation/inactivation ← V, Vt (fast HH kinetics)
- **n**: delayed-rectifier K⁺ activation ← V, Vt
- **p**: M-current (slow K⁺) gate ← V, V_half_p (param_i), tau_max_p (param_j); voltage-independent tau for stability
- **r**: I_h (HCN) gate ← V; fixed half-activation −80 mV, fixed tau 200 ms; corrects resting potential distribution
