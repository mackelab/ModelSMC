- **V** ← Na current (m³h), K current (n⁴), leak, M-current (p), injected current, noise
- **m, h** ← voltage-dependent Na activation/inactivation; fast kinetics via α/β rates
- **n** ← voltage-dependent K activation; intermediate kinetics via α/β rates
- **p** ← slow non-inactivating M-type K⁺ activation; Boltzmann steady-state, slow τ_p
- **Assumptions**: single-compartment, Hodgkin-Huxley kinetics; M-current regularizes tonic firing via subthreshold adaptation without bursting
