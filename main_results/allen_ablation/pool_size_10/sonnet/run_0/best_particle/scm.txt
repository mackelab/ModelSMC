- **V** (membrane voltage): driven by Na⁺, K⁺, leak, M-current, and injected current
- **m, h** (Na⁺ gates): voltage-dependent activation/inactivation via α/β kinetics
- **n** (K⁺ gate): voltage-dependent delayed-rectifier activation
- **p** (M-current gate): slow voltage-dependent K⁺ activation; promotes tonic regular firing
- **Assumptions**: single-compartment, Hodgkin-Huxley formalism, conductance-based currents with exponential integration
