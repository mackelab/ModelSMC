- **V** (membrane potential): driven by Na⁺, K⁺, leak, M-current, injected current, and noise
- **m, h** (Na⁺ gates): voltage-dependent activation/inactivation, fast kinetics
- **n** (K⁺ DR gate): voltage-dependent activation, intermediate kinetics
- **p** (M-current gate): slow non-inactivating K⁺ activation; half-activation at `param_i`, max time constant `−param_j`
- **Assumptions**: single-compartment, tonic spiking regime; M-current provides adaptation without bursting
